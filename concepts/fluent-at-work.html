Builder’s notes: why this page is built this way
  • The level check is the entry offer: low effort for the visitor, and it qualifies by level.
  • The hero shows the real situation the buyer fears, a meeting, and the result they want, so the benefit is visible in two bubbles.
  • Price and start date are stated up front on this concept; a live page could also hold them until after the check.
  • The goal field routes the follow-up message.
