Builder’s notes: why this page is built this way
  • One offer, one action. There is no navigation and every button scrolls to the same form.
  • The hero shows the deliverable itself, the one-page plan, so the visitor sees what they walk away with before reading a word.
  • The free fit call lowers the barrier. Price is stated before any commitment.
  • The 'not for you' list filters weak leads before they reach the calendar. Three form fields only; team size doubles as a qualifier.
