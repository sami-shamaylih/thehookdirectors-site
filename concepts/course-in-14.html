Builder’s notes: why this page is built this way
  • One goal: registration. The price is zero, so the words go to the outcome.
  • The pass shows date, time, length and price at a glance. The 14-day roadmap makes the promise concrete instead of abstract.
  • 'Will I be sold something?' is answered up front to protect trust and lead quality.
  • The topic field qualifies the lead and gives the host a personal opening line.
