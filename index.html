About

Media buying and creative in one team.

The Hook Directors is a performance advertising studio. The people who make your ads and the people who run them work together, so the idea and the numbers are never strangers.

How we work

A core team. A network of specialists.

The Hook Directors is a remote-first studio. We build a team around each project: media buyers, editors, AI video creators, shooters, copywriters and analysts, brought in for what the brief needs.

That keeps us fast, keeps quality high, and lets us scale up for a launch without carrying a team you don't need.
